Ladybug

Written by Eve

SCROLL

Leila, a skinny twenty year old black girl is washing a cup on the sink,

the hissing sound of a kettle at the background.

While she does this, she's talking to the walls...

Leila

Me? Do it? No I...I possibly could not.

Even though I want to... I can't, I'm not strong enough...

She turns and places the cup on a tray.

Then places a small plate on the same tray,

opens a cabinet, takes out a packet of two scones and closes the empty cabinet.

Leila

Oh dear, these won't be enough will they?

Well, you know how the economy is....but... if you ask me I think this is good...

you know...us girls, we aren't supposed to be eating a lot.

We have to maintain a slim figure you know?

She drops the scones on the plate.

Leila (cont'd) Oh I love coffee, it's a diuretic. It has caffeine so you don't really get hungry...actually coffee doesn't dehydrate the body, even though caffeine makes you pee more, there's a higher water content in your cup of coffee than the amount your body loses...

Cigarettes? No I can't smoke cigarrettes the witch won't let me.

By now the kettle is hissing to it's fullest, then suddenly it clicks off.
Which brings Leila back to the present.
She then takes the kettle and pours the boiling water on the cup that was on the tray.

Then carrying the tray, she walks out of the kitchen.

Leila appears in the bedroom holding a tray.

Delilah

What took you so long?

Leila places the tray on the bed.

An old sick woman is lying on the bed.
Delilah has eczema and a couple of radiation scars on her skin.
Her thin brown hair barely covers her head.

Leila

I was waiting for the kettle to boil the water.

Delilah

Come fix this pillows.

Leila goes ahead.

Delilah

Help me up...

Bring that tray here...

Does this have sugar?

Leila

YES!

Delilah

How many tablespoons?

Leila

Two!

Delilah

How many tablespoons of coffee did...

Leila

One!

Delilah

Why do these scones not have jelly on them?

Leila

We ran out of jelly.

Delilah

What? When?

Leila

Two weeks ago.

Delilah takes a sip of the coffee.

Delilah

This is too hot, why didn't you wait for it to cool down.

Leila rolls her brown eyes.

Leila

I guess I forgot.

Delilah

You guess you forgot?

Leila

Yes?

Delilah

That forgetfulness of yours is the reason you keep failing in school...

Leila

(whispers to herself) School?

Delilah

This scones are fucking tasteless, just like the bread before.

I've experienced three wars in my lifetime, counting this one
and in every single one of them, the breads are always tasteless.

It's as if all the good bakers get taken down
and only the amateurs are left to feed us.

Leila

Those are the last scones.

Delilah

What do you mean?

Leila

We have run out.

Delilah

Why didn't you tell me this?

Leila

I did, yesterday, and the day before... and the day before.

Delilah

Huh... (a long pause)

Well I don't need it anyway.
It won't cure me from dying.
Maybe I should stop eating altogether and quicken the process...
you'd love that wouldn't you?

Leila

Love what?

Delilah

My God you are a slow child...
if i knew how slow you'd be
I would have left you at that orphanage...

Leila looks hurt.

Delilah

...sorry that was mean...
I'm just...hateful towards life...
not you...never you.

Leila

Yeah...

Delilah

Here do you want some?

She hands her the plate which has the leftover piece from the first scone,
then takes the next piece for herself.
Leila takes the scone and eats it.
After a moment, she takes the plate to the kitchen.

She places the plate on the sink.

Leila Okay, I'll tell her...She might agree, we don't have the money...we can't go on like this... I am 20 years old, and that means I'm an adult and I can perfectly handle a job...

Besides when have I ever been irresponsible ....That was a long time ago. She won't say no
Delilah (O.S.)

LEILA!

Leila

YES!

She quickly runs to the bedroom.

Delilah

Open the windows for me, I want to feel the sun for just one more day.

Leila opens the window. Cold air rushes inside, blowing the curtains aggressively.

Delilah

Close it, close it. God what a gloomy day...How many gloomy days have there been?

Leila

A lot...it's rainy season.

Delilah

I know it's rainy season, you think I don't hear the rain?

Leila

But it's not raining.

Delilah

Now it's not, but it will...(quiet) What's the day today?

Leila

I don't know, probably Monday?

Delilah

Urgh Monday...I hate it. Even though I'm not going to work today...What a gloomy day.

Leila sits at the carpet, she's trying to find the courage to speak up.

Leila

Uhm.. I was thinking that... maybe I should find a job. You know, to at least put food on the table?

Long pause. Delilah looks at Leila expressionless then...

Delilah

HAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA!

Leila

Why are you laughing?

Delilah

YOU? WORK?

Leila

Yeah!

Delilah

Like in a job?

Leila

Yes...I can handle a job.

Delilah

OH really? Like what job? Tell me what job are you going to do.

Leila

WEELLL...

Delilah

Also tell me where you are going to work from? And who's going to pay you.

Leila

No I wasn't talking about...well obviously I won't be paid money...I was thinking of volunteering at one of the centres and maybe they could give us food in exchange.

Delilah

Oh...that sounds very smart, yeah! But the problem isn't the job, the problem is, do you have the courage to do it? Can you even talk to someone else who isn't me? Can you even look them in the eyes while talking to them, or will you stammer like always?

Leila goes quiet. She doesn't have an answer to that. She doesn't know if she really has the courage to do it. A minute passes. Delilah has now finished the last scone and her coffee.

Delilah (cont'd)

Go add me some more coffee and one scone.

Leila

There's no more scones, mom.

Delilah closes her eyes.

Delilah

GOD, when will the war end?

Leila

It already did.

Leila takes the tray to the kitchen as Delilah goes quiet, half awake.

Leila Well, I tried didn't I?

What am I going to eat?..... Yes I know I said that but now I'm hungry!

She opens some more cabinets, they are all empty. She makes coffee for herself with the remaining hot water and starts drinking it.

Outside? Nooo...It's raining...You know I wonder, If I go outside, will she remember I did?... Yes she'll forget. I could ran away and I won't be in trouble coz she might even forget about me, right?...no....if I do that then she'll die alone. All alone. That's really sad and I don't want that for her.
Delilah (O.S.)

LEILA!

Leila rolls her eyes then runs to the bedroom.

Delilah

Where's my coffee?

Leila looks guilty. She looks over to the kitchen and back to her mother.

Leila

There's none left.

Delilah

I'll be damned.

Leila stands at the door awkwardly as the silence falls again. Then Delilah shifts her gaze to her.

Delilah

Why do you talk to yourself child?

Leila

What?

Delilah

You talk to yourself. I always hear you mumbling things in the kitchen.

Leila

Oh. No. No I don't talk to myself.

Delilah

Do you have imaginary friends that you talk to?

Leila

Imaginary friends?

Delilah

Yes.

Leila

No, no I don't have friends. Imaginary friends.

Delilah

Then who are you talking to when you're in the kitchen? Are you mad?

Leila

I didn't know I was talking to someone. It's just something I do, you know, like make a story in my head and pretend I'm in the story.

Delilah

So, you are mad!

Leila

No, I'm not.

Delilah

Okay come here child...

Leila slowly walks closer to her.

Delilah

Closer... lay beside me...

She does, awkwardly.

Delilah

Do you know why I picked you that day?

Leila

Because our names rhyme?

Delilah

Yes Delilah and Leila rhyme,

She smiles, Leila is now lying on her laps.

Delilah

I remember when I first saw you...You were just five and you had these big eyes and of all the kids, you were the one who didn't smile at me. And I thought, what's this little shit's fucking problem? Does she not know that I'm the one who can save her from the pitiful life she's about to have? Then I realized, she's just five, what does she know about life. I asked you what your name was and you said Leila, but I can call you whatever I like. What did I say to you?

Leila

I will call you Leila, because I like it and it rhymes with my name.

Delilah

And then you asked me what my name was.

Leila

And you said DELILAH.

Delilah

That shocked you and you said, 'DELILAH? LIKE THE WOMAN WHO CUT SAMSON'S HAIR?', you were so adorable. I told you no, I told you that was a fable. you didn't understand of course, but I had already decided that from that day I would be your mother...

She goes quiet as if she forgot what she was planning to say. Leila gets bored and speaks up.

Leila

Mom, I want to go outside maybe I'll find something to eat.

Delilah

Why do you want to eat?

Leila

I'm hungry?

Delilah

Why are you hungry?

Leila

I don't know mom, I JUST AM!

Delilah

You are hungry because you want to stay alive.

Leila

I think I just want pancakes.

Delilah

No listen...you never listen to me child....Why do you want to stay alive? Why do you want to go outside? There's nothing there. There's nothing but death there.

Leila

But the voluntering centre has no death there. It has food and there's people and...

Delilah

I know you find that exciting, God knows how much trouble you gave me when I took you there...

Leila

(quickly interrupts) Please take me again, I promise this time I'll behave and I won't refuse to leave, please please!

Delilah

You know I don't have the strength to.

Leila

Then I can...

Delilah

No stay here with me... That place seduces you with food and you think that's what life is about, but it's not. It's just illusion, it masks the death that's underneath it and calls it survival. Do you like staying alive?

Leila

Yes?

Delilah

Really? What is it about life that you like so much? Is it this pathetic world we live in? Is it how you are stuck here and you can't leave? Is it that your mother is dying of cancer and you have to take care of her? Do you like that you have no friends, and not enough food?

Leila stands up from the bed, angry.

Leila

STOP! I know what you are trying to do! You don't want me to leave because you want me to die here with you and I wont!

Delilah

Nooo...that's not what I want! A mother wants nothing but life for her child...But there is no life in this world, my dear. The radiation took all of it. Have you ever seen an elephant? Have you ever seen fish? What about flowers? And don't you say you've seen them on movies coz it's not the same.

Leila's face is shedding tears now.

Delilah

Okay don't cry, there's no need to cry...

Leila continues to sob.

Delilah

How would you like to watch tv? huh?

Leila slowly nods.

Delilah

I know you like that...come on set it up.

Leila slowly but excitedly goes to where the tv is and switches it on. She takes out a folder with cds and next to it there's headphones and a music player. She takes one of the cds and puts it in the player. She joins her mother in the bed as the movie starts.

Delilah

How many times have we seen this movie?

Leila

A lot...I love it.

Delilah

You know everyone who acted in the movie is dead.

Leila

Everyone?

Delilah

Not a single one is alive.

Leila

Mom...why do you always talk about death?

Delilah

Coz it's all there is now.

Leila

But it's not, you're still alive, I'm still alive.

Delilah

We are not alive darling, we just have our eyes open.

Leila

I don't...

Delilah

When you talk to your imaginary friends or have those fantasies in your head, do you think that's what's happening in the present moment? In the real world?

Leila turns away from the tv and looks at her mom.

Delilah

Do you think those thoughts in your head are real? Or are you just making them up?

Leila is confused.

Delilah

You think life is like the fantasies in your head? You want to stay alive so that you can keep having these fantasies.

Leila

No...I don't have fantasies.

Delilah

Oh my poor child.. You are mad and you don't know it...you are mad. You have nothing but thoughts constantly swimming in your head, you make up stories and create ideas that aren't even real... Tell me, can you stop your mind from thinking?

Leila

Thinking?

Delilah

Yes.

Leila

Like stop thinking?

Delilah

Yes!

Leila

I can't! That doesn't even make sense.

Delilah

Why does it not make sense, can you do it or not?

Leila

Well, of course I can't. Thinking is what humans should always do. They should think before doing something or saying something.

Delilah

What about when you think too much? Do you think you should stop?

Leila

See, you just asked me if I think. Meaning I can't stop thinking.

Delilah

Okay, fine you can't stop thinking...but what is it you are thinking about?

Leila

Right now?

Delilah

YES.

Leila

I don't know, nothing I guess.

Delilah

So you don't know what you are thinking yet you can't stop thinking?...Okay so who is thinking, who is doing the thinking?

Leila

I'm the one thinking, it's my brain.

Delilah

Are you sure? What about when you're talking with your imaginary friends? Is that you doing the thinking or is there someone else.

Leila gets quiet and contemplates, the tv noise is now in the background.

Delilah

You are mad. You talk to yourself, you live in a fantasy. You can't control your thoughts and you think that your thoughts and perception of life are the truth but they are not....can't you understand? You are pretending to be alive, you are pretending to be someone you're not, you're pretending to have friends you don't have. You're pretending to be in a story that isn't real....It's all fake! All those thoughts, all those ideas, all those stories, they aren't real.

Leila isn't reacting, she looks confused. It's as if she's never considered this before.

Delilah

You don't believe me? You think I'm a crazy woman who just always forgets everything...I forget because there's no point in remembering, there's nothing to remember...it's all empty, it's all noise, none of it is real...

She looks at leila who isn't moving.

Delilah

Leila, where is your mind right now...

Leila doesn't speak.

Delilah

Won't you speak?

Leila

I...I don't know...

Delilah

I wish I could live long enough to see the day you would understand what I'm talking about, but how can you, you are just a child.

A few minutes pass and Delilah falls asleep. Leila sits there, the noise on the tv muffles the background. She finally wakes up and goes to the kitchen.

Leila She is crazy, I mean what was that about?...Death always talking about death, if she wants it so bad...

She stops herself as she gasps in realizaton.

I'm talking to myself...there's no one here...I'm talking to no one...

There's nothing happening now... I'm just talking to myself, I'm just imagining it.

Suddenly the tv noise goes silent, and an eerie quiet settles in. Leila suddenly gains an awareness, she starts to look around her and for the first time she notices the details in the kitchen. The faded white wood that furnished its cabinets. The black kettle that contrasted the white marble sink. The glass plates on the sink. She becomes aware of her body and starts looking at it, from her feet to her hands. To the botched skin she hated so much. The room suddenly gets vivid and she notices the window curtains were closed. She walks toward them, touches their dusty fabric and parts them wide. A bright light illuminates from the window. And she gets so entranced by it, she sheds a tear. She gets this desire to drink it, to drink the light.

Suddenly, she hears a bird singing, and the life that's outside her window. The buildings are rampaged, and the city is abandoned in post war architecture. She sees a red ladybug in a corner somewhere and gets fascinated by it. The breeze touches her skin and she shivers in a way that looks pleasurable. She closes her eyes and another desire comes. She wants the breeze to carry her to wherever it's going.

She opens her eyes after the breeze leaves. An awareness creeps in, we see it in her eyes. She moves away from the window and walks to her mother's bedroom, carrying that same awareness.

She stops at the door, she looks around the room, at the tv, at the bed. She looks at her mother for the first time. She really looks at her.

Leila

MOM?

Delilah opens her eyes. Leila walks closer but stops just before reaching the bed.

Leila

I'm leaving!

Delilah

Not this again!

Leila

No! I'm serious.

She walks towards her.

Leila

I finally understand what you meant...

She sits down and looks at her mother straight in the eyes. This startles Delilah.

Leila

I have been fantasizing about life, it was all in my head. My dreams, my thoughts, my feelings...

Delilah

What has happened?

Delilah looks at the new person sitting next to her. She has these big eyes that looked like they just witnessed a miracle.

Leila

I'm leaving you...You are dying and you seem to be fine with that. But I don't want to die, I want to live. And yes I have never seen an elephant coz there's no life out there but just now I saw a ladybug and it's the most beautiful thing I have ever laid my eyes upon. I know there's not much out there, but if something like that ladybug is out there then that's where I want to be. I want to live and see many more ladybugs and I can't do that here.

Delilah

Stop this nonsense.

Leila

I AM SERIOUS! THIS ISN'T NONSENSE. I AM LEAVING AND YOU WILL NEVER EVER SEE ME AGAIN!

Delilah

AND WHERE WILL YOU GO?

Leila

ANYWHERE THAT'S NOT HERE!

Delilah

YOU'RE FUCKING DELUSIONAL

Leila

I DON'T CARE!

She screams, like all her breath came out with those words. The echo of her scream takes its time in the room, then disappears. Delilah softens.

Delilah

Okay, if you want to go to the volunteering center, you can go. I have given you permission. Bring lots of bread, not the scones.

Leila looks confused, her outburst hasn't cooled off yet.

Leila

Really?

Delilah

Yeah...Just make sure you come back before night falls.

Leila stands up excited. She hugs her mother.

Leila

OH thank you thank you thank you!

Delilah hugs her back, smiling. Leila breaks the hug and starts to leave.

Delilah

Before you go, can you apply that lotion on me.

Leila looks at the lotion standing next to her mother's bedside table. This is the lotion they all apply to help neutralize the effects of the radiation. She fights the urge to walk out and picks up the lotion instead.

She begins applying the lotion to her mother's skin.

Delilah

Since you are going to the center, do you remember the way there?

Leila

I think so.

Delilah

You don't remember. Aren't you going to get lost?

Leila

I won't.

Delilah

What if the center is closed?

Leila stops applying the lotion. She looks at Delilah and finally understands what was happening. She gets up and distances herself from her.

Delilah

What now?

Leila starts pacing around the bedroom, trying to find the words that best describe her feelings.

Leila

You are a selfish person...

Delilah

What?

Leila

You are a selfish person, you're so selfish....You don't want me to go. Well I will and you can't stop me. I'm leaving and I'm never coming back!

There's a pause as Delilah tries to understand the situation.

Delilah

You want to abandon me?

Leila

Yes! You want to die, I'm not going to...

Delilah

(interrupts) Why don't you just kill me? Kill me and get your freedom.

Leila

What? Why are you...

Delilah

No go ahead, Kill me. I have given you permission. Kill me. Do it, God will forgive you. In fact who am I kidding, he doesn't care if you do it. So go ahead, kill me and go to that place you like so much.

Leila walks to the door. Delilah gets irrational and starts shouting at her.

Delilah

Kill me you fucking coward!

Leila turns around and walks towards her.

Leila

You know what I just realized...you didn't pick me because our names rhymed or you liked me and wanted to care for me. You picked me because you didn't want to live alone, and what better way than to adopt an unwanted child from the orphanage right?

Delilah

I picked you coz you needed me, you needed a mother.

Leila

You were never my mother, I was just a maid to you, a companion you can order around and tell stories about your past to. You were never my mother!

Delilah is in shock, there's a creeping block in her emotions and she can't remove it. She can't believe this is happening.

Delilah

You won't survive out there.

Leila

Let me worry about that...

Leila walks to the door but stops and turns around again.

Leila

you know...now that I think or now that I see things more clearly, I don't know why I was so afraid. Of you, of outside and of life. Nothing's ever happened to me, yet I have always been scared and anxious. I'm still alive after all this time, all the things I have feared, none of them ever happened, all the things I've wished for, they never happened. It was all in my head. It was all in my head!

Delilah

YES! It was all in your head! That's what I have been telling you all this fucking time. But just because you finally know that, doesn't mean you have the power to live life the way you want it. You don't, you are weak, life will swallow you up and spit you out, over and over again...Oh you think because the illusion has gone, life will suddenly start to favor you? Because you're aware now? Because you're enlightened?

Leila

I don't know what you're talking about and I don't care.

Delilah

Of course you don't, you're just in the beginning. I'll tell you what, you can go, you can leave. Go have a life... And one day, you will realize that I was never your enemy, I was never the one holding you prisoner.

Leila

You can't talk me out of it. I know you think I hate you but I don't.

Delilah

Bullshit! You think I've never heard you debate with yourself whether or not you should kill me?

Leila is taken aback!

Delilah

Haha! You mad child, your echoes reach this room all the time...But you know what, I wanted you to do it. God how I waited for you to do it, to take me out of my misery, if I had the strength to move my hands I would have done it already. Unlike you, you are such a fucking coward, you'd rather wipe my shit than put a knife to my chest.

Leila feels a weird emotion swelling inside her chest. She doesn't know what it is.

Delilah

And somehow you think you can survive out there? When you can't do something as simple as that? When you can't even slay the witch who is the only mother you'll ever have? That's right, I'm the only mother you'll ever have, because the one who gave birth to you, didn't want you. And If I'm honest, I don't blame her, look at you. You're an ugly child. I bet if she knew how ugly and mad you'd turn out to be, she wouldn't have given birth to you in the first place.

The emotion that was swelling in Leila's chest, like a lightning bolt, ascends to her head and takes away every thought in her brain. Then it takes over her body, and she moves to the bed, jumping on top of it. She yanks one of the pillows and with a strength only seen in animals, she places the pillow over the witch's face. She pushes the pillow down and down. Her mother's frail body fights back but Leila continues pushing. Down and Down. The body keeps moving. Down and Down she pushes. Down and Down until finally the witch no longer moves.

The emotion subsides as quickly as it had ascended, and Leila's eyes are no longer blinded by it. She takes away the pillow from her mother's face.

The witch's dead face doesn't surprise her. It still has the same dead hazel eyes that always looked down on her. Leila gets up from the bed.

Leila

You wanted to die...so there...you're welcome.

She walks away to the door and through the corridor to her room.

She opens her wardrobe. There's a suitcase there. She lifts the suitcase up and rolls it on the floor, walking to her late mother's room.

Leila

You don't know how long I've been waiting for this.

She looks at her dead mother once more. She tries to feel something in her heart, but just like her head, there's nothing there. Her eyes catch the headphones and the music player that's next to the tv desk. The movie in the tv is now showing the credit sequence. She walks to the desk and picks up the headphones and music player. It was an old one, a gift she got at the orphange from someone she no longer remembers. She puts the headphones on and connects the wire to the player. She scrolls through the catalogue of music displayed on the small device and presses play on one of them.

The music starts, she walks to her mother, takes a blanket and covers her body. Then she takes her suitcase and drags it out of the apartment. The outside looked the same way as she had always seen it from the window. But now it felt different. She begins walking away from the old house she had called home for over a decade. The music is almost at a climax and Leila can feel it in her bones. The freedom in her bones. As she walked further away and into the unknown, she started dancing and singing to the music. Laughing, Jumping.

She almost looked mad while doing it.

It was all in my head.
